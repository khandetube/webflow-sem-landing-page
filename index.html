06 / Engineering layer

Complex motion. Controlled system.

The interaction engine uses normalized scroll progress, sticky scene choreography, velocity-based skew, pointer parallax, spring-like interpolation, staggered reveals and reduced-motion handling. No animation is left to chance.

motion-engine / runtime.js
const progress = scrollY / maxScroll;
scene.pin("intent", progress);
scene.depth(cards, progress * 1.8);
scene.morph("signal", smooth(progress));
scene.horizontal("motionRail", progress);
pointer.tilt(".tilt-card", {max:9});
velocity.skew(".rail-card", scrollDelta);
reveal.stagger("[data-reveal]");
motion.respect("prefers-reduced-motion");
